Jovia Network · Information for members

What is Jovia Network?

Jovia Network is a digital platform designed to let members access entertainment and other platform activities that may qualify for rewards under the applicable programme rules. Depending on availability and eligibility, activities may include watching celebrity videos, participating in games, listening to music, engaging with shopping features, selling products and other activities published on the platform.

How it works

  • Choose a membership: Review the available Silver and Gold packages, their fees, features and terms before registering.
  • Complete registration: Provide accurate information and follow the instructions shown on the official platform.
  • Participate in eligible activities: Follow each activity's instructions, time limits and eligibility requirements.
  • Review your rewards: Where the platform provides a rewards balance, check the displayed records and applicable withdrawal conditions.
Reward rates, availability, eligibility, processing times and withdrawal requirements are governed by the current official programme rules. Promotional examples should not be treated as a guarantee of earnings.

Our approach

We aim to present platform features, membership differences, fees and reward conditions clearly so that members can make informed decisions. Features may change as the platform develops, and some features may be limited by location, account status, availability or applicable law.

Using Jovia responsibly

Members should review all package details before paying, keep their account information secure, use the platform lawfully and avoid misleading claims about rewards or results. Do not register or pay based on unofficial messages or promises that are not stated in Jovia's official terms.

Questions or support

For questions about membership, account access or platform activities, use the support/contact channel published on the official Jovia Network website or inside your account, if available.
